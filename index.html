03

How it works

From enquiry to report, in five stops

Ask, Agree, Label, Analyse, Receive: follow one sample along the bench, or move it yourself with the numbered stops.

Prepare your samples
  1. 1

    Stop 1: Enquiry & quote

    Tell MCL your material, question and preferred tests, then agree the quotation and completion date.

  2. 2

    Stop 2: Form & label

    Complete form F3-MCL (revision 3, 18 June 2026) and label every container with its sample ID.

    Illustrative label; not a laboratory tracking number.

  3. 3

    Stop 3: Delivered & logged

    Deliver as agreed; every container must match its entry on the form’s sample-ID sheet.

  4. 4

    Stop 4: Instrument

    Your sample is tested by the method MCL confirmed for your request.

  5. 5

    Stop 5: Report

    Receive raw data, a report or both; a report is compulsory for accredited testing.

How to send a sampleDownload form F3-MCL (PDF) (opens in a new tab)

Completion dates are agreed case by case. Prepare a sample only after MCL confirms the method. Do not send biohazardous material.
