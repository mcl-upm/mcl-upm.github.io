01Privacy and personal data

Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) notice — draft for institutional approval. UPM to approve applicable legal basis, responsible data controller, notice languages, effective date and complete institutional privacy wording before launch.

This notice describes the planned website features. verify actual analytics settings, form ownership and data flows against this notice before publication.

02What you choose to provide

An enquiry may include your name, organisation, contact details, testing question and selected tests. Workshop interest and feedback forms ask for information needed for those requests. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information or confidential sample details. approve the final fields and identify which are required.

Information provided through these routes is intended for responding to your request and handling the service or workshop enquiry. approve purposes, access permissions, recipients and retention periods; confirm whether any further use applies.

03Website counting and optional analytics

GoatCounter: cookieless visitor counting is planned to run without optional-analytics consent and supply the public website counter. It counts site use, not laboratory clients or test submissions. confirm the account, collected fields, hosting, retention and public-counter settings.

Google Analytics 4: GA4 is planned to run only after you select Accept. Select Decline to leave GA4 off. You can revisit the choice through Privacy choices in the footer. verify consent controls and disclose the configured GA4 data, storage, recipients and retention before launch.

04Quote basket

Your quote basket is stored only in this browser using browser storage. It is not automatically sent to MCL and does not follow you to another device. You can remove items or clear the basket. A printout or saved PDF remains wherever you choose to keep it.

When the quotation form is available, its pre-filled link will contain selected tests only, with no personal data in the URL. Review what you submit. Until then, the button opens an email enquiry.

05Google Forms and external links

Enquiry/quote, feedback and workshop-interest forms are planned as MCL-owned Google Forms. Their links open a separate service. publish the approved form links, identify their owners and disclose relevant processing, recipients and any overseas transfers. Until forms are ready, enquiry actions open email.

Map, payment and accreditation links also open external services. Review their notices before supplying information.

06Feedback and publication

Permission to publish a testimonial is a separate optional choice. Sending feedback alone does not give publication permission. confirm approval, withdrawal and removal arrangements for published comments.

07Your choices and contact

Use Privacy choices for optional analytics and Clear basket for locally saved tests. For questions about personal information you have submitted, email kkamcl1595@upm.edu.my with the subject MCL privacy enquiry. confirm the authorised privacy contact and process for access, correction, withdrawal and complaints.

Notice effective: approved publication date. Retention and deletion: approved schedule. Security and disclosure conditions: institutional wording.
