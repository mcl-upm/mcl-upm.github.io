01Access to information

The website is being designed towards WCAG AA, with keyboard access, readable text, image descriptions and layouts that work on smaller screens. complete accessibility testing and publish the assessed standard, version, date and known limitations; do not claim conformance before assessment.

02Reading and navigation

Use Skip to content to move past the header. Search is labelled . Pages and diagrams should retain their information when enlarged or read without animation. verify keyboard order, focus visibility, zoom, contrast and screen-reader labels before launch.

See these controls on the panel above ↑

03Motion and illustrations

Animations are intended to respect your device’s reduced-motion preference and show their final state. Each signature illustration should have a useful text equivalent. Decorative traces must not be mistaken for measured data. verify motion, diagram alternatives and keyboard interactions in the finished site.

04Documents and video

Downloadable documents and future video should include readable text, clear headings, captions and suitable alternatives. assess the supplied PDFs and future video; list any inaccessible files and available alternatives.

05Ask for help

If you cannot use a page or download, email kkamcl1595@upm.edu.my with the page address, what you were trying to do and the format you need. confirm accessibility-support owner and response arrangements; no response time is promised.

This statement concerns the website. For physical access, parking or a laboratory visit, contact MCL before travelling. confirm physical-access information separately.
