02

Before you pack

Sample preparation by family

Prepare only after MCL confirms the method. There is no published universal minimum quantity or container specification in the supplied records.

  • Crucible pan

    Thermal

    Describe the material and the transitions or mass changes of interest; confirm mass, temperature programme, atmosphere and whether drying is appropriate.

  • ATR crystal

    Spectroscopy

    Describe solid, powder, film or liquid form. For accredited FTIR-ATR, confirm an eligible polymer that is a non-adhering solid or non-volatile liquid; do not assume all FTIR preparation routes are accredited.

  • 2 mL vial

    Chromatography

    Identify the compounds of interest, solvent, expected concentration and available standards; confirm extraction, dilution, filtering and container requirements.

  • Specimen stub

    Microscopy

    Describe surface or cross-section, conductivity and features of interest; ask before cutting, coating, mounting or drying.

  • Sample tube

    Surface & particle

    State whether the material is powder, bulk solid or dispersion; confirm drying, degassing, dispersion medium and suitable quantity.

  • Digest flask

    Elemental

    State the target elements and sample matrix; confirm digestion, preservation and whether preparation is included. CHN and sulphur are separate brochure lines.

  • Sampling bottle

    Water & other

    State the requested measurements and sample origin; confirm responsible laboratory, container, preservation and delivery timing before collection. Water requests on the form are assigned to the Environmental Engineering Laboratory.

  • Not accepted: biohazardous material.

    Do not send biohazardous material. The supplied profile excludes biohazardous samples; another supplied profile also excludes radioactive and explosive materials. confirm the final acceptance and prohibited-material policy before publication.

MCL to approve family-specific preparation instructions, quantities, containers, hazard exclusions and sample-return/disposal rules.
