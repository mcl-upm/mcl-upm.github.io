MCL recognised under the ETS accreditation framework

August/September 2026 post — exact date confirm

The department congratulated MCL on accreditation under Engineering Testing Services. The announcement links to the Standards Malaysia schedule for SAMM 1265. Its chemical-testing scope is qualitative polymer identification by FTIR-ATR for non-adhering solids and non-volatile liquids. Other tests are outside the accredited scope. Read the full scope before requesting an accredited test or citing accreditation in your project.
