What it tells you

Also available on request:

NIR/FIR. Explore near- and far-infrared response for material comparison.

Light microscopy. See visible structures, particles and fibres to investigate product quality.

i-Raman. Compare vibrational bands to investigate bonding and material phases.

Bomb calorimetry. Measure combustion heat to compare the energy content of fuels and feedstocks.

How it works

Inside each option, step by step

  1. NIR/FIR: infrared light interacts with your sample. Detected absorption or reflection reveals its spectral response in the selected region.
  2. Light microscopy: illumination and objective lenses form a magnified image of a prepared surface or transparent specimen.
  3. i-Raman: a laser illuminates your sample. A spectrometer resolves the small energy shifts in scattered light into vibrational bands.
  4. Bomb calorimetry: a weighed specimen burns in an oxygen-filled sealed vessel surrounded by water. The corrected temperature rise gives the heat released during combustion.
