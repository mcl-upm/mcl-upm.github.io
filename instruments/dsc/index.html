What it tells you

See how your material responds to heating and cooling. DSC reveals glass transitions, melting, crystallisation and curing through changes in heat flow. Use it to compare formulations, investigate processing history or choose thermal conditions for product development. A transition's position and shape help you understand behaviour under the selected test conditions.

Questions it answers

  1. Q1At what temperature does my material melt?
  2. Q2Where is my polymer’s glass transition?
  3. Q3Has my resin finished curing?
  4. Q4How has processing changed my thermal behaviour?

How it works

Inside the DSC, step by step

  1. A small portion of your sample sits in one pan; a reference occupies the other position.
  2. Both follow a controlled heating or cooling programme.
  3. Sensors track the difference in heat flow between them. Melting absorbs heat; crystallisation and curing can release it.
  4. The curve builds against temperature or time. A glass transition usually appears as a baseline step, while melting and crystallisation produce peaks. Comparing heating cycles helps distinguish processing history from repeatable material behaviour. Peak direction follows the instrument's sign convention.
