What it tells you

See where your sample absorbs ultraviolet and visible light. UV-Vis helps you compare colour-related compounds, track reactions and measure suitable known analytes using calibration. A scan reveals absorption bands; a selected wavelength supports targeted measurements. Clear samples, a matched blank and suitable chemistry make concentration comparisons meaningful.

Questions it answers

  1. Q1At which wavelengths does my sample absorb light?
  2. Q2How concentrated is my calibrated target analyte?
  3. Q3Has my sample’s absorbance changed after treatment?
  4. Q4Do my batches have similar absorption spectra?

How it works

Inside the UV-Vis, step by step

  1. A light source supplies ultraviolet or visible radiation, and optics select the wavelength.
  2. Light passes through your solution in a cuvette. Absorbing molecules reduce the transmitted beam.
  3. The detector compares transmission with a blank and calculates absorbance. Scanning wavelengths builds a spectrum.
  4. Standards relate absorbance to concentration over a suitable range. Solvent, path length and chemical form must match the method. Cloudiness scatters light, and overlapping bands can obscure individual components in mixtures.
