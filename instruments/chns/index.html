What it tells you

Measure the carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen or sulphur in your material. Combustion analysis helps you compare feedstocks, check formulations and track changes during treatment. Results express bulk elemental content on a defined sample basis. They complement molecular identification by showing how much of each measured element is present.

Questions it answers

  1. Q1How much carbon does my feedstock contain?
  2. Q2Do my batches have consistent elemental composition?
  3. Q3Has treatment changed my nitrogen content?
  4. Q4How much sulphur is in my material?

How it works

Inside the CHNS, step by step

  1. A representative portion of your sample is weighed into a capsule.
  2. High-temperature combustion with oxygen converts its elements into gaseous products.
  3. A carrier gas moves these products through conditioning stages. Depending on the analyser, gases are separated or measured by selective detectors.
  4. Calibration relates each signal to an elemental amount. Dividing by sample mass gives the elemental percentage. Moisture and sample homogeneity affect comparisons, so use the same reporting basis across batches. Oxygen content needs a separate measurement or calculation.
