Impact & sustainability.

Reliable testing depends on careful use of resources, capable people and clear responsibilities. This page sets out MCL’s environmental, social and governance priorities, the UN Sustainable Development Goals they relate to, and departmental STEM outreach. Proposed improvements are distinguished from evidenced activity; no measured impact is claimed. MCL to approve current practices and provide dated supporting records.

01

ESG · E

Environmental

Use resources carefully.

Priorities proposed for review include chemical tracking, appropriate solvent choices, segregated waste, safe instrument power-management schedules and digital records. No reduction in energy, water, paper or waste is claimed here.

02

ESG · S

Social

Support people who use and run the laboratory.

MCL’s priorities cover safety, technical development, approachable customer support and workshops for researchers. Published workshop announcements provide examples of learning opportunities.

03

ESG · G

Governance

Make the scope and decisions clear.

Publish the defined accredited scope, distinguish other tests, show dated prices and provide a route for questions. Further priorities include impartiality, confidentiality and controlled testing records.
