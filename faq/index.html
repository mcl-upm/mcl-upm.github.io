§ Tests and accreditation

Q03How do I choose a test?in answer

Use the Test Finder to describe your material and question, then ask MCL to confirm suitability.

TestsLink to Q03

Q04Which tests are accredited?in answer

Only qualitative FTIR-ATR identification of polymers within the SAMM 1265 scope: non-adhering solid and non-volatile liquid polymers, 400–4000 cm⁻¹, using the stated method. Other FTIR work, and every other test including DSC and AAS, is outside that scope; historical SAMM 711 records are separate. See the complete wording on Quality & Accreditation.

AccreditationTestsLink to Q04

Q05Where can I verify accreditation?in answer

Read the Standards Malaysia SAMM 1265 schedule.

AccreditationLink to Q05

Q06Does accreditation certify my product?in answer

The schedule defines a testing scope; it does not provide a general product-approval claim. Discuss the intended use of your report with MCL.

AccreditationLink to Q06

Q07Can I see example results?in answer

obtain approved, anonymised MCL examples. Any public reference example must be labelled with its external source and must not be presented as an MCL result.
