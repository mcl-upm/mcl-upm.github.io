Quality & accreditation

Quality you can verify.

Scope schedule

01

Before you ask for it

What accreditation means for you

Check three things before requesting an accredited test: your material, the question to be answered and the stated method. The current scope is qualitative identification of the specified polymer samples. It does not accredit every instrument, identify every compound in a mixture or certify a product’s fitness for use.

Ask for Accredited · SAMM 1265 only when your request falls within that scope. Other FTIR work, DSC, AAS and all other catalogue tests are Outside accredited scope. An accredited test requires a report.

In scope only when all three pass

  1. 01 · Material

    Polymer, as a non-adhering solid or non-volatile liquid

    Not Mixtures to be separated, metals, powders that stick

    Pass

  2. 02 · Question

    Qualitative: what polymer is this?

    Not How much of each component; fitness for use

    Pass

  3. 03 · Method

    FTIR-ATR, 400–4000 cm⁻¹, UPM/FK/ETS/TMD 1 (ASTM E1252-98 R2021)

    Not Any other instrument or method

    Pass

02

From SI units to your report

Traceability chain

Follow the connection from measurement references to a clearly identified report. The five-link illustration explains the quality framework; the actual references and controls depend on the method. MCL to confirm method-specific traceability records and wording before publication.

03

Objective and discreet

Impartiality and confidentiality

Your question deserves an objective assessment. Tell MCL if a commercial relationship, competing interest or confidentiality condition needs attention before work is agreed. approve current impartiality policy, conflict-of-interest procedure and reporting contact.

Please discuss confidentiality requirements before sending sensitive formulations, personal information or unpublished results. approve confidentiality commitments, permitted disclosures, access controls and retention periods; link the current policy.

04

Earlier scopes

Accreditation history

History 19 May 2015

19 May 2015 — SAMM 711. Historical material records DSC and AAS accreditation associated with MCL and the faculty’s strength laboratory. This is an earlier scope.

Historical DSC/AAS scope does not establish current accreditation.

History 2021

2021 — pandemic interruption. The historical and current awards are separate records.

Historical DSC/AAS scope does not establish current accreditation.

Current 7 August 2026

7 August 2026 — SAMM 1265. The current ETS FK schedule covers the defined FTIR-ATR polymer-identification service until 7 August 2031.
